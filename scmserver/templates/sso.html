Let your users sign in through your organization's identity provider — Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta, Auth0, Keycloak or any other OpenID Connect provider. Signing in with a password keeps working.

Shown on the sign-in button.
The provider's issuer, not its sign-in page. The server reads /.well-known/openid-configuration beneath it. Must be an https address reachable from the internet.
{# The stored secret is never sent to the page. Blank = keep it. #}
The first time someone signs in, they are matched to their OpenSCM user by email address. Normally only an address the provider marks as verified is accepted. Some providers never say so — Microsoft Entra ID, by default — and then nobody can be matched. Tick this to accept the address anyway. Only do so if the provider is your own company directory, where administrators set email addresses. If people can choose their own address at the provider, leave it off: someone could enter a colleague's address and be signed in as them.
The provider is contacted when you enable it; it is only switched on if it answers correctly.
